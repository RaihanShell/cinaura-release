# Cinaura – Android TV Beta Releases

This repository contains **official signed APK releases** of the Cinaura Android TV app, made available for **beta testing via sideloading**.

Cinaura is built for people who want to **own and control their media library**, regardless of where their files are stored.

---

## What is Cinaura?

Cinaura is a personal media library app for Android TV.

It lets you bring together content from multiple sources (local network, cloud services, debrid services and more) into **one clear, calm interface** that belongs entirely to you.

Nothing is injected.  
Nothing is recommended.  
Nothing appears unless you add it.

Your library exists because you decided it should.

---

## Who is this beta for?

This beta is intended for:
- Android TV users who manage their own media
- People comfortable with sideloading apps
- Early adopters who enjoy testing unfinished products
- Anyone who values ownership, clarity, and control over their media

This beta is **not** for:
- Users looking for streaming subscriptions
- People expecting a fully polished, bug-free experience
- Devices without Android TV support

---

## How to install

1. Download the latest APK from the **Releases** section.
2. Transfer the APK to your Android TV device.
3. Enable **Install unknown apps** on your device.
4. Install the APK using a file manager or sideloading app.
5. Launch Cinaura from your app list.

> The APKs in this repository are **signed by the official developer**.
